FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a roof repair or a full roof replacement?

It depends on the age of the roof, the extent of the damage, leak history, and the condition of the surrounding shingles and roof system. Elevated Exteriors can inspect the roof and explain whether a targeted repair or replacement is the more practical option.

Can you inspect my roof after hail or wind damage?

Yes. If your home was recently hit by hail, high winds, or heavy rain, schedule an inspection to look for missing shingles, bruising, lifted areas, flashing problems, and other signs of storm-related roof damage.

How soon should I call if I see a roof leak?

Call as soon as possible. Even a small leak can lead to insulation damage, drywall stains, mold concerns, and structural issues if left unaddressed. For urgent roofing concerns, call (317) 500-3154.
