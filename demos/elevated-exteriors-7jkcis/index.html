How do I know if I need a roof replacement?

Common signs include missing or damaged shingles, recurring leaks, visible wear, sagging areas, storm damage, or a roof that is nearing the end of its useful life. A professional inspection can help determine whether repair or replacement is the better path.

Do you work with homeowners after storm damage?

Yes. Elevated Exteriors can inspect your roof after wind, hail, or severe weather and explain the damage found, the urgency of repairs, and the best next steps for restoring your home’s protection.
